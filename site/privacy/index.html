Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

TrackWorth ("the app") is a free application for desktop computers (macOS, Windows, Linux) and Android, for manually tracking account balances and net worth. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. In short: your data stays on your device and in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives it.

1. No servers

TrackWorth has no backend server, database, or account system operated by the developer. The app runs entirely on your device and communicates directly with Google (for sign-in and Google Drive, and on Android, Google Play for app updates), GitHub (for update checks on desktop) and Frankfurter (for exchange rates). The developer cannot see, access, or recover your data.

2. Information accessed from your Google Account

When you choose "Sign in with Google", you are asked to grant these permissions. On desktop this happens in your web browser; on Android, in Google's own account picker provided by Google Play services. The permissions are the same on both:

  • Basic profile: name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile). Used only to show who is signed in, for example your name in the app's header. Your name and email are kept on this device so the app can show them when you open it offline; they are deleted when you sign out and are never sent anywhere else.
  • Google Drive, files created by the app only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). Used to create, read and update the app's own files in a My Drive/TrackWorth folder, which hold the accounts and balances you enter: settings.json, accounts.json, and one file per financial year in TrackWorth/balances. With this permission the app cannot see or access any other files in your Google Drive.

3. Information you enter

Account names, tags, liquidity ratings, balance sources, currencies, balances, dates and notes that you type into the app are saved in the TrackWorth folder in your Google Drive, as readable JSON files, plus a local copy on your device (section 4). You can open, download, edit or delete those files at any time with Google Drive.

4. Information stored on your device

  • Desktop: a Google refresh token, which keeps you signed in between launches, is stored in your operating system's secure credential store (macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, or Linux Secret Service). It is removed when you sign out.
  • Android: no token or password is stored by the app. Google Play services remembers the access you granted and gives the app short-lived access tokens when needed. The app keeps only a small marker that you are signed in, which is removed when you sign out.
  • A local copy of your TrackWorth files is kept in the app's private storage on your device, so the app starts quickly, only downloads files that changed, and can show your data when you're offline. Changes you make while offline are kept here until they're uploaded to your Google Drive. It is never sent anywhere else, and it is deleted when you sign out (and when you sign in again, in case it's a different account). Your Google Drive stays the master copy.
  • Short-lived access tokens are held in memory only and discarded when the app closes.
  • Your theme choice, downloaded exchange rates and any rates you enter yourself are kept on the device. They contain no personal or financial data.

5. What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting to third parties.
  • No connection to banks, brokers, or other financial services.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No use of your information to develop, improve, or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • No human access to your data: the developer has no copy of it.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

TrackWorth's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the features described above, which are visible to you in the app, and is never transferred to third parties.

7. Update checks

On Android, updates are delivered by Google Play, under the Google Privacy Policy; the app itself makes no update checks. On desktop, to offer new versions, the app periodically downloads a small version file from GitHub (github.com), and update packages when you approve an update. Like any website, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. No personal or financial data is sent in these requests.

8. Third-party services

Your use of Google sign-in, Google Drive, Google Play and Google Play services is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Exchange rates. When you have accounts in more than one currency, or open the Currencies page, the app downloads published exchange rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev), a free, open-source service that republishes central-bank reference rates. These requests contain only currency codes and dates. No balances, account names or Google account details are sent, though the service necessarily sees your IP address. Rates, and any rates you enter yourself, are kept only on your device.

9. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. On desktop, Google sign-in happens in your own web browser using the OAuth 2.0 PKCE flow; on Android, through Google Play services. The app's interface has no direct internet access and can only reach the specific Google endpoints, the update feed and the exchange-rate service it needs. App updates are cryptographically signed and verified before installation. Exports you save go only where you choose: on Android, through the system "Save to…" picker, which gives the app access to that one file only. The Android app asks for no storage, contacts, location or other device permissions beyond internet access.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Your data files: stay in your Google Drive until you delete them. Delete the TrackWorth folder in Google Drive to remove all app data.
  • Sign-in and local copy: choose "Sign out" in the app to revoke access and delete the stored token and the local copy of your data. You can also remove TrackWorth's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes it from your device. On desktop, sign out first to remove the stored token from your credential store and the local copy of your data. On Android, uninstalling also deletes the app's local copy of your data; to remove TrackWorth's access to your Google Account, sign out first or use the link above.

11. Children

TrackWorth is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
