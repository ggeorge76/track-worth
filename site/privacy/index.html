Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

TrackWorth ("the app") is a free desktop application for manually tracking account balances and net worth. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. In short: your data stays on your device and in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives it.

1. No servers

TrackWorth has no backend server, database, or account system operated by the developer. The app runs entirely on your device and communicates directly with Google (for sign-in and Google Drive) and GitHub (for update checks). The developer cannot see, access, or recover your data.

2. Information accessed from your Google Account

When you choose "Sign in with Google", you are asked to grant these permissions:

  • Basic profile: name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile). Used only to show who is signed in, for example your name in the app's header. This information is kept in memory while the app runs and is not stored or sent anywhere else.
  • Google Drive, files created by the app only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). Used to create, read and update a single file, My Drive/TrackWorth/trackworth-data.json, which holds the accounts and balances you enter. With this permission the app cannot see or access any other files in your Google Drive.

3. Information you enter

Account names, categories, currencies, balances, dates and notes that you type into the app are saved only in the data file in your Google Drive. You can open, download, edit or delete that file at any time with Google Drive.

4. Information stored on your device

  • A Google refresh token, which keeps you signed in between launches, is stored in your operating system's secure credential store (macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, or Linux Secret Service). It is removed when you sign out.
  • Short-lived access tokens and your loaded data are held in memory only and discarded when the app closes.

5. What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting to third parties.
  • No connection to banks, brokers, or other financial services.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No use of your information to develop, improve, or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • No human access to your data: the developer has no copy of it.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

TrackWorth's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the features described above, which are visible to you in the app, and is never transferred to third parties.

7. Update checks

To offer new versions, the app periodically downloads a small version file from GitHub (github.com), and update packages when you approve an update. Like any website, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. No personal or financial data is sent in these requests.

8. Third-party services

Your use of Google sign-in and Google Drive is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Google sign-in happens in your own web browser using the OAuth 2.0 PKCE flow. The app's interface has no direct internet access and can only reach the specific Google endpoints it needs. App updates are cryptographically signed and verified before installation.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Your data file: stays in your Google Drive until you delete it. Delete the TrackWorth folder in Google Drive to remove all app data.
  • Sign-in: choose "Sign out" in the app to revoke access and delete the stored token. You can also remove TrackWorth's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes it from your device. Sign out first to remove the stored token from your credential store.

11. Children

TrackWorth is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/ggeorge76/track-worth/issues.
